Keep the stable mission ID, candidate-pair mapping, scoring and save semantics, but replace the generic “excursion morning slot” with one of three complete stories selected automatically from the saved pair:

The child never sees pair administration, “TBC”, route selection, manual confirmation, tickets, parking or a request for an adult to invent anything. The logistical pair choice remains in Parent Corner; once saved, the app opens the corresponding story directly. The existing rotation engine, not a parent, assigns any retained role labels.

Each route below supplies its opening, actions, answers, retries, ending, offline equivalent and narration. The only real-world observations are optional mirrors of an app-led puzzle; cards are not a lesser mode.

## What is weak in the current mission

- “Franja de excursión de la mañana” is database language, not a story title.
- The opening explains selection, mission IDs, scoring, roles, manual confirmation and skipping before anything happens.
- Every variant asks an adult to choose a route, room, viewpoint, sign or pattern. The app relinquishes its job as guide.
- “Find one shape” and “explain what repeats” are prompts, not authored adventures; the family must create the interesting part.
- The Sigurtà copy barely uses its strongest verified fact: this is a **polirinto** of more than 1,500 yew plants, with multiple exits and a central tower.
- The Sirmione copy lists defensive shapes but misses the dramatic purpose of the fortified dock and its relationship with the lake and north wind.
- The museum route says “fossil, animal, or shape” without using Verona's world-famous Bolca collection or its almost fifty-million-year time depth.
- Safety and fallback prose repeatedly interrupts the story. Logistics belong in Parent Corner; a single contextual cue is enough where a live hazard exists.
- The generic success line “Ruta de mañana resuelta” gives no narrative payoff.

## Evidence boundaries

- **HECHO:** the polirinto's design and plants; the castle, harbour and documented function; the museum's Bolca collections and fossil ages.
- **INTERPRETACIÓN INSTITUCIONAL:** the dock's irregular form may have protected its inner water from the `pelèr`. Retain “pudo” in Spanish.
- **NUESTRA HISTORIA:** a stolen compass direction, talking green shadows, an attacking wind, the three-part shield and a fossil fish sending a message.
- Do not promise flowers, animals, maze entry, arrival at the tower, castle entry, dock access or one particular object being on display.
- Do not say that Verona itself was underwater fifty million years ago. The fossils come from the Bolca deposits in the province; the app says a vanished sea is preserved in **Bolca's stone**.

### Display title

> Al cruzar la puerta del jardín, la aguja verde de Bussola se divide en mil quinientas sombras. Todas susurran direcciones distintas. Para recuperarlo no hay que correr ni llegar al centro. Basta descifrar tres señales verdes: **una Y, un muro vivo y una torre**.

## Variant B — `sirmione-castle-water`

### Display title

**El puerto que engañó al viento**

### Opening narration

> Bussola llega a Sirmione y descubre un castillo que no termina en la orilla: sus murallas abrazan un pequeño puerto dentro del lago. De pronto sopla el **pelèr**, el viento del norte, y roba tres piezas de su escudo: **una almena, un muro y una gota de agua**. En nuestra historia, si reconstruimos el dibujo antes de que el viento dé tres vueltas, las embarcaciones de papel volverán a encontrar refugio.

Primary CTA: **Cerrar el puerto de papel**

The “before three turns” line is dramatic, not a timer. No countdown, speed requirement or failure state is introduced.
