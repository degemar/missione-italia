# R4 research — `ISL-03` · El hilo que dibuja en el aire

**Mission:** `ISL-03` · **Research date:** 1 October 2026 · **Runtime edited:** no

## Recommendation

Replace the current colour-matching and truth-sorting checklist with a complete, narrated Burano mystery. A white thread catches on Bussola's needle, loses the stitch that lets it draw without cloth, and releases three story keys: **NIEBLA**, **LÍMITE** and **ESPUMA**. The app tells each account, asks what kind of truth it is, reveals how real lace can form its own structure, and lets the children repair the tiny rectangular net characteristic of Burano stitch. The restored thread then draws an original digital flower and unlocks Creativity.

The app supplies the story, order, role rotation, visual prompts, answers, hints, retries and ending. No adult chooses a house, assigns a child, invents dialogue, judges an observation or creates a fallback. The whole mission works from cached illustrations; seeing Burano's façades is an optional atmospheric bonus, not evidence required for completion.

Keep one contextual cue only if the optional outdoor window opens: **“Las fachadas se observan desde el camino; la pista continúa en la pantalla.”** Show it once, without narration. Do not put instructions about adults, phones, residents, walking or photography inside the story, quiz, retries or celebration.

## Existing-copy audit

- The current opening explains procedure before anything happens to Bussola.
- “El Vigía… El Detective… El Guía…” reads like task allocation, not storytelling.
- The adult must choose the stopping place, so the product leaves a creative and operational gap.
- Matching two arbitrary colours and shapes does not teach anything distinctive about Burano.
- Sorting `HECHO`, `TRADICIÓN` and `LEYENDA` is a good learning goal, but it is presented as schoolwork rather than a mystery whose answer changes the story.
- The lace line is only decorative; the app never explains what lace is or what makes Burano stitch visually distinctive.
- The strongest existing fact—the 1872 school—appears after the activity and has no narrative role.
- Four fallback paragraphs and two warning sentences dominate a twelve-minute mission that should feel light and magical.
- The mission depends on an outdoor observation even though every documented idea can become a richer illustrated interaction.
- The success line announces Creativity without letting the children repair or create anything.

## Evidence and language caveats

- Treat the fishers-and-fog account as **tradition**, not proven history. The Comune uses the phrase *tradizione popolare*.
- Treat the foam veil as **legend**. Do not narrate the distant war as a verified event or imply that one named couple founded the craft.
- The property-boundary explanation is the Comune's present official account, but the page is not a cited archival study. Child copy should say **“el relato oficial del Comune explica…”**, not “historians proved…”.
- Bright façades are homes and part of a living community. Original app art may evoke a row of varied façades but must not reproduce a recognisable private house, resident, shopfront or photograph.
- Do not claim Burano invented lace. MUVE describes a wider Venetian and European history; the mission is specifically about Burano stitch and its school.
- “Encaje sin tela” means the finished lace does not depend on a woven textile backing. Real needle lace still uses a drawing, temporary tacking, a cylinder and a cushion while it is made. Never portray the craft as literally floating thread or as effortless magic outside the fictional layer.
- Do not turn **rectangular ladder rungs** into a universal definition of all lace. They distinguish the net background of Burano stitch in the MUVE glossary.
- Do not call bobbin lace the Burano stitch used in this puzzle. MUVE classifies Burano stitch as needle lace.
- The school dates are `1872–1970`; the School Museum opened in `1981`. Do not collapse those into “the museum was founded in 1872.”
- The museum's live demonstration is conditional on opening hours. Never promise that a lacemaker will be present.
- The municipal page names Cencia Scarpariola in its nineteenth-century revival account, while MUVE's school page refers more cautiously to an elderly surviving teacher. Keep the name out of the core story unless a later editorial pass reconciles the two accounts.
- Current opening times, admission, queues and transport are deliberately outside this mission and require separate live visitor checks.

## Story direction

### Display title

**El hilo que dibuja en el aire**

Keep `ISL-03`, `lagoon-islands`, order `10`, power `creativity`, reward stamp `story-sleuth`, scoring, completion states and offline-critical status unchanged.

### Opening narration — 89 words

> En Burano, la aguja de Bussola atrapa un hilo blanco que no está cosido a ninguna tela. El hilo intenta dibujar una flor en el aire, pero solo consigue hacer tres nudos: **NIEBLA**, **LÍMITE** y **ESPUMA**. Son las llaves perdidas de una escuela de encaje marcada con el año **1872**. Cada llave guarda una historia distinta y solo una red muy especial puede mantenerlas unidas. Descubrid qué clase de historia cuenta cada nudo, reparad el punto de Burano y el hilo recordará cómo convertir el vacío en dibujo.

Primary CTA: **Seguir el hilo**

The opening is fiction; `1872` is the documented date embedded in it. The narration never asks anyone to allocate work, find an unspecified real object or decide what happens next.

### Visual grammar

- A hand-cut paper Burano skyline uses invented façades: turquoise/circle, saffron/star and coral/triangle. Every colour has a persistent shape and text label.
- Bussola remains a brass compass with no face or limbs. Its needle catches the white paper thread and points to the next card.
- The lace is a high-contrast white thread over dark lagoon blue, with a thick instructional trace suitable for a four-year-old.
- The `1872` door is a paper archive cover, not a realistic reconstruction of the museum or school.
- The three truth keys use both words and silhouettes: `HECHO` = document/square, `TRADICIÓN` = speaking spiral/circle, `LEYENDA` = moon/star.
- No animation depends on colour, sound or motion alone. Reduced motion replaces unfurling thread with three still construction frames.

## Complete authored play sequence

Target duration: six to eight minutes. All required illustrations, captions and answer logic ship inside the offline content package. The current roster rotation may display a child's name before each scene, but it deals the action automatically and offers **Ayudar juntos** on every screen.

### Scene 1 — El armario de colores

The archive cover shows three invented façade cards. A fixed ribbon displays the code; it never disappears:

1. **TURQUESA + CÍRCULO**
2. **AMARILLO + ESTRELLA**
3. **CORAL + TRIÁNGULO**

Narration:

> Antes de abrir los nudos, el hilo necesita encontrar su camino entre los colores. Vigía, toca las tres fachadas siguiendo el código que aparece en la cinta. El color da una pista y la forma confirma la respuesta.

Interaction: three large tap targets. The child taps in the displayed order; there is no memory test, drag precision or timer.

Per-tap response:

- correct: the selected shape becomes a white thread loop and the next code pulses once;
- wrong: **“Esa fachada guarda otra forma. Busca ahora el {shape}.”** The fixed code remains visible;
- third correct: **“Código completo. El armario de historias está abierto.”**

This colour code is original game fiction. It must not be described as Burano's official palette or imply that every real house uses one of these colours.

### Scene 2 — Las tres llaves de historia

The app opens one knot at a time. Each account is narrated and captioned before three persistent answer keys appear.

#### Knot 1 — NIEBLA

> Se cuenta que los colores ayudaban a los pescadores a reconocer su casa al volver entre la niebla del invierno. El Comune lo llama una tradición popular. ¿Qué llave abre este nudo?

Correct choice: **TRADICIÓN · voces que pasan una historia**

Kind retry: **“La propia fuente dice que es una tradición popular: una historia transmitida, no una prueba.”**

Resolve: **“NIEBLA abre la llave de la Tradición.”**

#### Knot 2 — LÍMITE

> El relato oficial del Comune explica que el arcoíris de fachadas es relativamente reciente y que los colores ayudaban a marcar los límites de pequeñas propiedades. ¿Qué llave corresponde?

Correct choice: **HECHO EN LA FUENTE · documento que podemos comprobar**

Kind retry: **“Esta explicación aparece como la versión factual de la fuente oficial; busca la llave con el documento.”**

Resolve: **“LÍMITE abre la llave del Hecho.”**

Editorially, retain `HECHO` in the existing taxonomy but narrate “hecho en la fuente” or “relato oficial comprobable” so the app does not overstate the page as archival proof.

#### Knot 3 — ESPUMA

> Una leyenda imagina que la espuma levantada por un remo se convirtió en un velo de encaje para la persona que esperaba en la orilla. ¿Qué llave necesita?

Correct choice: **LEYENDA · relato maravilloso**

Kind retry: **“La espuma que se vuelve velo pertenece a un relato maravilloso: busca la estrella de Leyenda.”**

Resolve: **“ESPUMA abre la llave de la Leyenda.”**

After all three: the keys become thread spools labelled **lo que se transmite**, **lo que dice la fuente**, **lo que imagina una leyenda**. This reinforces the distinction without a score screen.

### Scene 3 — Un dibujo sin tela

The thread reaches the archive door but falls through a square hole.

Narration:

> Las tres llaves son correctas, pero aún no forman encaje. En la obra terminada, el hilo debe sostener su propio dibujo: no se queda cosido sobre una tela tejida. Durante el trabajo puede seguir un diseño y apoyarse en herramientas; al final, la estructura del hilo permanece. ¿Qué imagen puede convertirse en encaje?

Three large illustrated choices:

| Choice ID | Illustration and accessible label | Correct | Response |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `thread-own-structure` | White thread loops connected into a self-supporting motif; “El hilo forma su propia estructura” | yes | “Exacto. Puntada a puntada, el hilo construye el dibujo.” |
| `painted-fabric` | Flower painted on a solid woven square; “Una flor pintada sobre tela” | no | “Eso decora una tela, pero el dibujo no está construido por el hilo.” |
| `paper-print` | Flower printed on paper; “Una flor impresa en papel” | no | “El papel muestra un dibujo; el encaje necesita que el hilo forme la estructura.” |

The illustration may show a temporary pale guide fading after the answer, but it must not suggest that real lace is made without tools or preparation.

### Scene 4 — La escalera de Burano

The archive door opens to three background samples.

Narration:

> El Museo del Encaje guarda una pista visual: el fondo del punto de Burano forma pequeñas mallas rectangulares, parecidas a peldaños de una escalera vertical. Una de estas redes ha perdido un peldaño. Guía, encuentra primero la red de Burano y después repara el hueco.

#### Step A — Identify the net

| Choice ID | Accessible illustration | Correct |
|---|---|---:|
| `burano-rectangular-rungs` | Narrow vertical column of tiny rectangular meshes; “Rectángulos como peldaños” | yes |
| `venice-honeycomb` | Six-sided honeycomb field; “Malla de hexágonos” | no |
| `solid-checker` | Solid woven checkerboard; “Tela de cuadros cerrados” | no |

Retry: **“Busca rectángulos pequeños colocados uno sobre otro, como los peldaños de una escalera.”**

Resolve: **“Red encontrada: los peldaños rectangulares distinguen el fondo del punto de Burano.”**

#### Step B — Repair one missing rung

Show a five-rung column with rung four absent. Three pieces appear below: a matching narrow rectangle, a hexagon and a filled square. The child taps the rectangle; it snaps into the gap. Avoid precise dragging.

Retry: **“El hueco tiene dos lados largos y dos cortos. Elige el rectángulo que continúa la escalera.”**

Resolve: **“Peldaño reparado. La red ya puede sostener el dibujo.”**

### Scene 5 — La flor del aire

The repaired net unfolds behind the three truth keys. Their icons become an original three-petal motif; the colour cards appear only as a paper-theatre background.

Final narration:

> El hilo ya recuerda. La Tradición conserva una voz, el Hecho conserva una fuente y la Leyenda conserva la imaginación. Ninguna llave es igual a otra, pero las tres caben en una red bien construida. En 1872 abrió en Burano una escuela para transmitir el arte del encaje. Hoy vuestro último peldaño completa una flor digital: no es encaje de Burano, sino un homenaje creado para la historia de Bussola. **¡Creatividad encendida!**

Celebration line: **“El hilo volvió a dibujar en el aire.”**

Reward: existing `story-sleuth` stamp and `creativity` power. The stamp art should combine a thread loop, a rectangular rung and the three truth-key silhouettes; it should not copy museum lace.

Optional discovery card after celebration:

> **Una escuela para no olvidar.** La Escuela de Encaje de Burano funcionó de 1872 a 1970. En 1981 abrió el Museo de la Escuela, que conserva diseños y piezas de varios siglos.

CTA: **Guardar en el pasaporte**. Secondary: **Ver cómo se pronuncia**.
