# R4 research — ISL-02 · El bosque que dibuja caminos

**Mission:** `ISL-02` · **Research date:** 1 October 2026 · **Runtime edited:** no

## Recommendation

Replace the current stop/route-label exercise with a complete app-led mystery about the wooden navigation markers of the Venetian lagoon. Bussola's map loses every road, but a rhythmic **toc, toc-toc-toc** reveals an alphabet rising from the water: the *briccole* mark navigable channels and a taller group called a *dama* announces a channel entrance.

The app tells the story, introduces every fact, supplies the observation prompt, runs two illustrated puzzles, provides its own fallback and delivers the ending. Nobody assigns roles, selects a symbol, invents dialogue, validates a child's answer or makes up the continuation.

Use only one contextual cue, woven into the story immediately before looking outside: **“El barco se convierte en mirador. Sin levantaros del asiento, buscad madera que nace del agua.”** Do not repeat water, boarding, phone or adult-control instructions in the story, quiz, reward or celebration.

Keep real transport planning outside the game. ACTV routes, departures, landing stages and service changes are operational information and must continue to come from ACTV's current map/timetable, not from the offline mission.

## What is weak in the current mission

- The current `storyBeat` is mostly a disclaimer about what children cannot choose.
- Three assigned roles turn the family into game administrators instead of story participants.
- “The destination card chosen by the adult” leaves the core creative decision outside the app.
- Matching generic stop, route and direction labels teaches little that is distinctive about the lagoon.
- The mission interrupts itself twice to explain transport responsibility before any mystery has begun.
- The completion line, “Símbolo de la laguna emparejado,” has no reveal, image or emotional payoff.
- Four fallbacks restate the same mechanical task rather than offering an authored alternate branch.
- The source supports real water transport, but the copy misses the far more memorable navigation system visible in the lagoon itself.
- The current title, **Guía de la laguna**, sounds like a utility screen rather than an adventure.

## Evidence and caveat boundary

- Use **briccola** in the singular and **briccole** in the plural. Treccani gives the standard Italian spelling with double `c`; Comune pages sometimes use the local/editorial variant `bricole`. Pick one spelling and stay consistent in the app.
- A briccola marks a channel edge or navigable route; it is not a “water traffic light,” and the app must not invent red/green meanings.
- A *dama* is the multi-post channel-entrance marker described by the Comune source. Do not claim that every cluster seen from the boat is a dama: other poles, moorings and marker arrangements exist.
- Teach and score the one-post, three-post and taller four-plus-post distinction only against the app's controlled illustrations. Real-world spotting is an unscored observation.
- Do not imply that the entire lagoon is deep water. The contrast between channels and surrounding shallows is the reason the marker story works.
- The `82 / 13 / 5` composition is an official landscape graphic, not a live measurement. Present it as “el mapa del Comune resume la laguna así,” not as a changing daily reading.
- Briccole can host lagoon organisms below the surface, but no child should be asked to identify a species from a moving boat.
- ACTV route numbers, stop letters, times and temporary changes are volatile. They do not belong in cached narration or scored choices.
- The story map from Murano's glass bead to Burano's bright-house stamp is a narrative chapter map, not travel guidance.

## Story direction

### Display title

**El bosque que dibuja caminos**

Keep `ISL-02`, `lagoon-islands`, `creativity`, `kind-navigator`, completion states and offline-critical status unchanged.

### Opening narration

> Al dejar atrás Murano, una gota salta sobre el mapa de Bussola. Las líneas azules se corren, las flechas se borran y, de pronto, no queda ni un camino. Entonces llega un sonido desde la laguna: **toc... toc-toc-toc**. No son árboles. Son letras de madera clavadas en el agua. Una voz cruje entre ellas: **“Si aprendéis nuestro alfabeto, el camino invisible volverá a aparecer.”**

Primary CTA: **Escuchar el toc-toc**

Visual grammar: an inked map washes pale; one wooden post stamps `TOC`, three bound posts stamp `TOC-TOC-TOC`. Do not show the completed channel or the *dama* yet.

## Complete playable sequence

The core path lasts five to six minutes. It requires no GPS, live transport data, role selection, parent prompt or knowledge outside the app.

### Beat 1 — The road that vanished

The app reveals a top-down lagoon illustration: blue-green water, pale shallows, two tiny island stamps and no connecting line.

Narration:

> Bussola busca una calle, un puente, hasta una raya de tinta. Nada. En la laguna, algunos caminos no están sobre la tierra. Hay que leer la profundidad del agua y las señales de madera.

Three sealed clue cards appear:

1. **UN PALO** — a single post rising from the water.
2. **TRES PALOS** — a tied group with three wooden heads.
3. **UNA CORONA ALTA** — four posts with one taller than the rest.

CTA: **Abrir el alfabeto de madera**

### Beat 2 — Meet Briccola and Dama Alta

The first two clue cards join into one character family.

> **Briccola** puede aparecer sola o en un grupo de tres. Marca el borde entre el canal más profundo y los bajos fondos. No dice “gira ahora” ni elige un barco: dibuja por dónde existe el camino de agua.

Then the third card grows one post above the others.

> Cuando cuatro o más palos se reúnen y uno se alza por encima, aparece **Dama Alta**. Su silueta anuncia la entrada de un canal.

The names are introduced before the quiz, so success depends on observation rather than prior knowledge.

### Beat 3 — Puzzle one: find the channel gate

**Prompt:** “¿Quién puede anunciar la entrada de un canal?”

| Choice ID | Card | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `single-post` | One post with a small wave | retry |
| `triple-briccola` | Three equal posts tied together | retry |
| `tall-dama` | Five posts, one clearly taller | correct |

**Correct response:**

> ¡Dama Alta! Cuatro o más palos, uno por encima del grupo. La primera puerta del mapa acaba de abrirse.

**Retry for `single-post`:**

> Esa briccola puede marcar el borde del camino. Busca ahora el grupo con una cabeza más alta.

**Retry for `triple-briccola`:**

> Tres palos forman una briccola. La guardiana de la entrada reúne al menos cuatro y levanta uno sobre los demás.

No wrong answer subtracts progress. The correct silhouette pulses once; audio is optional and never required to solve it.

### Beat 4 — Puzzle two: draw the invisible road

The app opens a cross-section/top-down hybrid illustration:

- pale green-grey = shallow water;
- deep blue ribbon = navigable channel;
- briccole = markers along both sides;
- a small folded-paper boat = Bussola's story token, not a real route planner.

**Prompt:** “Las briccole enseñan los bordes. ¿Dónde recuperamos la línea del mapa?”

The child drags a silver-blue ribbon through one of three illustrated corridors.

| Choice ID | Destination | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `between-markers` | Through the blue corridor between the two rows of briccole | correct |
| `across-shallows` | Across the pale shallow area | retry |
| `over-island` | Across the tiny green island | retry |

**Correct response:**

> ¡El camino estaba entre las señales! La madera no mueve el agua: hace visible el canal que la profundidad había escondido.

**Retry for `across-shallows`:**

> La tinta se ha escapado hacia los bajos fondos. Vuelve al corredor azul que protegen las briccole.

**Retry for `over-island`:**

> Ese sello es tierra. El camino secreto de esta historia está dentro del agua, entre los postes.

Reduced-motion mode replaces dragging with three large tappable route cards.

### Beat 5 — Open the real lagoon window

This is an optional observation, never a scored identification test.

Show the one contextual line:

> **El barco se convierte en mirador. Sin levantaros del asiento, buscad madera que nace del agua.**

Then dim the app and offer exactly two actions:

- **Hemos visto los palos**
- **Abrir la laguna ilustrada**

Both continue to the same reveal. There is no timer, role checklist, live-location check, photo request, parent validation or instruction to identify the arrangement.

If the first action is chosen:

> Toc, toc. Una letra del alfabeto de madera acaba de saltar del paisaje al mapa de Bussola.

If the illustrated branch is chosen:

> La ventana de papel también funciona. Briccola y Dama Alta vuelven a asomar entre ondas de tinta.

### Beat 6 — Seal the maker-island map

The app returns to its own fictional chapter map. A glass-drop stamp marks Murano; a bright-house stamp marks Burano. The newly discovered channel ribbon appears between them without a line number, timetable or real landing stage.

Narration:

> La cuenta de vidrio y la casa de colores estaban separadas por un espacio en blanco. Ahora el mapa sabe el secreto: en la laguna, un camino puede estar hecho de agua, profundidad y madera. Bussola no ha elegido ningún barco. Ha aprendido a leer el paisaje.

CTA: **Estampar el camino invisible**

This scene connects `ISL-01`, `ISL-02` and `ISL-03` narratively without pretending to navigate the family's real journey.

## Completion and reward

### Reveal narration

> Dama Alta levanta su poste mayor. Las briccole responden **toc-toc-toc** y estampan dos hileras de madera sobre el mapa. Entre ellas corre una cinta azul hasta la isla de las casas brillantes. La aguja de Bussola gira una vez y se queda quieta: **“La Creatividad no solo inventa cosas nuevas. También aprende a ver los caminos que otros ya supieron dibujar.”**

**Success line:** “El bosque de madera ha devuelto el camino invisible.”

**Stamp:** Keep `kind-navigator`; redesign it as a tiny blue channel passing between two wooden-post seals, with Dama Alta at the top.

**Discovery fact:** “Las briccole señalan rutas y bordes de canales navegables en la laguna; las dame, con cuatro o más palos y uno más alto, pueden marcar la entrada de un canal.”

### Optional curiosity card — The lagoon in 100 stamps

Animate 100 small paper stamps into three groups:

- 82 blue water stamps;
- 13 green-brown *velme* and *barene* stamps;
- 5 ochre emerged-land stamps.

Caption:

> **PARA MENTES CURIOSAS** · El mapa del Comune di Venezia resume la laguna como 82 partes de agua, 13 de velme y barene y solo 5 de tierra emergida. Por eso sus caminos de agua importan tanto.

### Optional curiosity card — The garden below the knock

Show one briccola above and below the waterline.

> **OTRO SECRETO** · Sobre el agua, la briccola es una señal. Bajo el agua, su madera ofrece una superficie firme en una laguna de fondos blandos, y allí pueden vivir organismos capaces de soportar el cambio de las mareas.

Do not depict or label a specific species unless it receives its own verified source.

## Fallbacks that preserve the story

- **No briccole visible / rain / tired child:** `Abrir la laguna ilustrada` plays the same observation reveal and continues to Beat 6. No manual explanation is required.
- **Not aboard yet:** begin with Beats 1–4 and use the illustrated branch for Beat 5. The mission remains complete.
- **No GPS:** irrelevant; the story never verifies position.
- **Changed ACTV service:** irrelevant; no real line, stop letter, departure, platform or boarding decision appears in gameplay.
- **Offline:** cache the washed-map scene, marker silhouettes, channel puzzle, island stamps, captions and narration in the app shell.
- **Reduced motion:** use static before/after panels, one-step opacity changes and tappable route cards; no drifting water, auto-drawing path or map rotation.
- **Audio unavailable:** show `TOC · TOC-TOC-TOC` as animated text stamps and preserve all information in captions.

Every fallback is an authored app branch. None asks a parent to invent, assign, judge or narrate anything.
