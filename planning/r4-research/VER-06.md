# R4 research — VER-06 · El puente de la aguja

**Mission:** `VER-06` · **Research date:** 1 October 2026 · **Runtime edited:** no

## Recommendation

Replace the current role recap with a narrated finale called **El puente de la aguja**. Bussola opens over an illustrated Adige and its needle falls out as five numbered pieces. Verona supplies the answer: Ponte Pietra is not one uniform object but five arches carrying material and work from different periods. After its destruction in 1945, original stones and bricks were recovered, ordered, numbered and reused in the reconstruction. Different pieces did not become identical; they were put back into relationship.

That is the emotional meaning of Teamwork for this family: **nobody had to do the same thing for the journey to become one story**.

The app assigns three authored turns automatically from the saved age-band profiles:

1. the `4–6` profile completes an arch by shape;
2. the `7–8` profile orders three Verona layers in time;
3. the `9–11` profile solves the recovered-stones mystery;
4. all three names illuminate the final bridge while Bussola says one Italian word: **insieme**.

Every prompt, answer, hint and line is supplied. There is no role picker, physical card, adult confirmation, invented story, perfect-score check or location task. The whole finale plays on one phone anywhere and lasts about four minutes.

## What is weak in the current finale

- It starts by explaining saved powers, roles, manual choices and skips instead of creating a final mystery.
- It asks the family to administer `Vigía`, `Detective` and `Guía` one more time. The adult still has to decide who does what and place cards.
- Its only fact is `NUESTRA HISTORIA`; `sourceIds` is empty even though Verona offers a documented, emotionally exact teamwork image.
- Labelling a favourite icon as `HECHO`, `TRADICIÓN`, `LEYENDA` or `NUESTRA HISTORIA` repeats the sorting lesson from `VER-02`. It does not synthesize the journey.
- `choices: []` leaves no real finale puzzle. Completion is a list of spoken or physical chores rather than an authored interaction.
- “Funciona en cualquier lugar seguro”, “el adulto puede colocar las tarjetas”, manual/skipped state explanations and four generic fallbacks expose implementation language inside the story.
- Both current `safety` lines describe product rules, not immediate hazards. They can disappear from the child flow.
- “Ruta distinta, mismo equipo” is a useful idea, but the app has not dramatized it, so the success line arrives unearned.

These chapter seals are visual callbacks only. A skipped or manually resolved mission never produces a blank, lock or accusation. The same four seals appear for every valid save state; resolved missions can add subtle texture, not determine access.

The current Verona sequence provides the right synthesis:

- `VER-01` introduces repeated Arena arches and Roman time.
- `VER-02` separates a medieval building, a literary story and the 1939–1940 balcony setting.
- `VER-03` introduces the five arches of Ponte Pietra and the Adige.
- `VER-04` and `VER-05` prove that Friday can branch without breaking the journey.
- `VER-06` should reveal why those differences belong together.

## Evidence boundaries

- **DOCUMENTED FACT:** dates, architectural layers, five arches, recovery and reuse of original bridge material.
- **LITERARY / PLACE TRADITION:** the Juliet association. It may appear as an illustrated page or balcony layer, never as proof that Juliet was a historical resident.
- **OUR STORY:** Bussola opening, the needle falling into an ink-river, speaking stones, glowing seals and the compass becoming a bridge.
- **SAVE-STATE LOGIC:** completed, manual and skipped clues are implementation state. Never narrate them as historical facts or child-facing exceptions.

The central metaphor is supportable because it is clearly framed as Bussola's fiction inspired by documented restoration. Do not claim the real bridge was rebuilt “by three children”, “to restore a compass”, or through magic.

## Complete deterministic sequence

### Prelude — Four pages, one journey

The Atlas flips through one seal from each chapter. The animation is short and never pauses for confirmation.

> Curiosidad hizo preguntas. Asombro encontró lo extraordinario. Creatividad convirtió pistas en patrones. Pero la cuarta luz no aparece con otra pista. Aparece cuando cosas distintas consiguen sostenerse unas a otras.

The app then calls the first saved nickname. Assignment is calculated, not delegated:

- first available `4–6` member receives Beat 1;
- first available `7–8` member receives Beat 2;
- first available `9–11` member receives Beat 3;
- ties use stable family order;
- missing profiles become `Turno 1`, `Turno 2`, `Turno 3`, and the app chains them without asking an adult.

### Beat 1 — The curve that holds · age band 4–6

**Call:** “{nickname}, la pieza más pequeña busca una curva.”

Three large silhouettes appear: an arch, a full circle and a zigzag. The gap in the bridge has the matching arch outline.

**Prompt:** “¿Qué forma completa el puente?”

| Choice ID | Visual | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `arch` | semicircular arch | correct |
| `circle` | full circle | retry |
| `zigzag` | lightning/zigzag | retry |

**Correct:**

> ¡Arco encontrado! La Arena y Ponte Pietra usan curvas que reparten el peso y dejan pasar el espacio por debajo.

**Retry:**

> Sigue el borde de la abertura: sube en curva y vuelve a bajar.

The fragment snaps in with a quiet stone sound. Shape and outline carry the answer; reading and colour are unnecessary.

### Beat 2 — Three layers of time · age band 7–8

**Call:** “{nickname}, Bussola ha mezclado tres momentos de Verona.”

The app teaches before it tests. Three illustrated cards arrive with their dates spoken and printed:

- Arena arch — **siglo I**;
- documented medieval house — **1351**;
- famous balcony setting — **1939–1940**.

**Prompt:** “Ordénalos desde el más antiguo hasta el más reciente.”

Correct order: `arena` → `house-1351` → `balcony-1940`.

**Correct:**

> ¡El tiempo encaja! Verona no borró todas sus capas: las dejó conversar.

**Retry:**

> Empieza por el número más pequeño: el arco romano llegó muchos siglos antes que la casa y el balcón.

Accessibility alternative: three numbered landing places accept one tap per card; no drag is required. This is a chronology puzzle, not a claim that Juliet was historical.

### Beat 3 — The stones in the river · age band 9–11

**Call:** “{nickname}, queda el secreto de las cinco piezas.”

> Ponte Pietra tiene cinco arcos de épocas distintas. Tras quedar destruido en 1945, muchas piedras y ladrillos originales fueron recuperados del Adigio, ordenados y numerados. ¿Qué permitió reconstruirlo con tanta memoria?

| Choice ID | Answer | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `recover-understand-reconnect` | **Recuperar las piezas, comprender su lugar y volver a unirlas** | correct |
| `make-identical` | **Cambiar todas las piezas para que fueran idénticas** | retry |
| `forget-old` | **Olvidar el puente anterior y esconder sus restos** | retry |

**Correct:**

> Exacto. Las piezas no tuvieron que volverse iguales. Cada una regresó donde podía sostener a las demás.

**Retry:**

> Pista: las piedras fueron recuperadas, ordenadas y numeradas. La memoria estaba en las piezas y en saber cómo se relacionaban.

The answer adds three small marks to the fragment: recover, order, reconnect. No points are lost and the other answers remain visible after the reveal.

### Beat 4 — The word in the centre

The five bridge fragments are now complete, but the compass centre remains dark. Each child receives one app-authored action in saved profile order:

1. “{nickname 1}, toca **Curiosidad**.”
2. “{nickname 2}, toca **Asombro**.”
3. “{nickname 3}, toca **Creatividad**.”

After each tap, Bussola places that light automatically. No cards or objects outside the phone are required. If a nickname is absent, the app says `Primera luz`, `Segunda luz`, `Tercera luz`.

Then Bussola supplies the final word:

> En italiano, **insieme** significa “juntos” y también puede nombrar un todo formado por varias partes. Yo diré las sílabas. El equipo solo tiene que tocar el centro cuando la palabra esté completa: **in… siè… me**.

The centre button unlocks after the spoken/visual syllables and accepts one tap from anyone. Pronunciation is never graded.

### Finale

> **Insieme.** El arco sostiene la piedra, la piedra sostiene la página, y cada luz encuentra su lugar. La aguja se levanta del río de tinta convertida en puente. Bussola por fin lo entiende: el Trabajo en equipo no significa hacer lo mismo. Significa que lo distinto puede avanzar en una misma dirección. Curiosidad. Asombro. Creatividad. Trabajo en equipo. **Cuatro luces, una brújula.**

The bridge folds upward into Bussola's needle. The chapter seals orbit once, then stamp the passport.

**Success line:** “Piezas distintas. Un solo viaje. Bussola vuelve a orientarse.”

**Stamp:** keep `team-spark`, redrawn as five small arches holding one compass star.

## Fallbacks integrated into the story

- **Anywhere / hotel / rain:** this is the normal path; no alternate copy.
- **Friday destination skipped or unresolved:** identical finale and full reward. The bridge story never names the chosen Friday branch.
- **No audio:** exact captions plus syllable highlighting; the centre unlocks on the same timer.
- **Reduced motion:** five static fragment cards become a completed bridge by cross-fade; no orbit, shake, parallax or flashing.
- **One or two saved profiles:** the app chains the three beats and renames them `Turno 1–3`; no setup interruption.
- **No saved profiles:** `Explorador 1–3`; the finale remains complete.
- **No drag precision:** every drag puzzle has a tap-to-place equivalent.
- **Offline:** package all artwork, captions, narration and answer feedback.
- **Wrong answer:** one authored hint, then the same prompt; no score, timer or sibling comparison.

No manual fallback needs to appear in child copy. The repository can preserve `manual` and `skipped` as recovery states outside the narrated path.

## Safety treatment

The proposed mission asks for no movement, exterior observation, public performance or object handling. Therefore show **no narrated safety copy and no safety card** inside `VER-06`.

If product policy requires one contextual label, use only a silent metadata chip on the launch screen: **`Final en pantalla · 4 min`**. It is not part of the story and should not be read aloud.

- The finale opens with a mystery and reaches an emotional payoff without an adult speaking, assigning, validating or inventing anything.

- The three challenges include answer, feedback and retry copy and remain playable without prior mission recall.
- Teamwork is demonstrated through Verona's documented layered bridge, not stated as a generic moral.

- No safety speech, operational instruction or implementation fallback interrupts the narrative.
- Fact, literary tradition and app fiction remain visibly distinct.
- Audio, captions, reduced-motion, tap alternatives and offline assets reach the same reward.
- The chapter ends with a specific image and line: five arches become one compass needle; **piezas distintas, un solo viaje**.
