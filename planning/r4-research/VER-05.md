# R4 research — `VER-05` · La página de las tres puertas

**Mission:** `VER-05` · **Research date:** 1 October 2026 · **Runtime edited:** no

## Recommendation

Replace the current administrative “afternoon slot” with three authored stories selected automatically from the already chosen excursion pair:

| `selectedPairId` | Morning partner | Active `VER-05` variant | Child-facing story |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sigurta-borghetto` | `sigurta-team-maze` | `borghetto-water-mill` | **El hilo amarillo del molino** |
| `sirmione-lazise` | `sirmione-castle-water` | `lazise-bonus-spotter` | **La ciudad que tenía tres lados** |
| `verona-rain` | `verona-natural-history` | `verona-childrens-museum` | **La chispa de las seis puertas** |

The parent may still choose the real itinerary in Parent Corner, but that is the last creative decision required. Once a pair is selected, the app opens the corresponding scene, assigns any saved family roles automatically, supplies every line and clue, evaluates every answer and delivers the ending. Nobody is asked to make up a symbol, choose a challenge, nominate a storyteller, validate what another person saw or decide when the activity is “enough.”

All three stories have a complete illustrated path and can be finished away from the destination. A live observation is an optional unscored echo near the end, never the engine of progression. Remove the generic role/safety/fallback recital from the child flow.

Only two contextual hazard lines are useful, each shown once and only if the relevant exterior echo is opened:

> **Borghetto:** La pista se mira desde el paseo; no os acerquéis al borde del Mincio.

> **Lazise:** En el puerto, observad desde el paseo.

The museum needs no safety narration. Its ticket, time-slot and socks information belongs in Parent Corner before departure, not inside Bussola's story.

## What is weak in the current mission

- `Franja de excursión de la tarde` sounds like a scheduling object, not a chapter in an adventure.
- The generic opening explains variant mechanics, role labels, manual confirmation and skipping before it creates any suspense.
- Every variant delegates authored work back to the family: choose a viewpoint, invent a team symbol, choose a discovery, explain a surprise, arrange an improvised sequence or decide with an adult when to stop.
- The three roles require a parent to assign and supervise them instead of the app calling the next explorer automatically.
- “Con el adulto,” “aprobado por el adulto,” “el adulto elige,” “dejad de caminar” and similar phrases interrupt almost every beat even where there is no immediate hazard.
- Borghetto contains a strong fact-versus-legend idea, but the current answer is printed in the objective and the task never becomes a dramatic mystery.
- Lazise is currently a generic shape hunt. It omits the excellent verified visual fact that walls enclose three sides while the lake forms the west side.
- The museum fallback asks the family to invent a beginning-middle-end story despite the app already knowing six verified science themes that can drive a complete sequence.
- Offline cards, manual completion and skipping are repeatedly described as story content. They are resilience controls and should remain outside narration.
- “Una pista basta” is a product rule, not an ending. Each branch needs a reveal that changes Bussola's map.

## Evidence boundaries and time-sensitive caveats

- The old mill wheels, stone houses, Ponte Visconteo and grinding of wheat/cereals are place-history claims. Silvia, Malco and the yellow silk cloth belong to a local **legend**. Bussola's trapped thread and speaking wheel are **NUESTRA HISTORIA**.
- Do not say the Lover's Knot tale proves how tortellini were invented. The official destination page presents a culinary legend, not documentary origin evidence.
- Do not imply that every visible mill mechanism is publicly accessible or still performing its historical function. No touching, entry or functioning machinery is required by the app.
- Do not encode current Borghetto parking, bridge traffic, works, crowd level or viewpoint access in the story. The Municipality has discussed changes and conservation around Ponte Visconteo; conditions are volatile.
- Use “murallas en tres lados y lago al oeste” for Lazise. Do not ask how many gates exist: official municipal descriptions use inconsistent counting conventions.
- Do not call Lazise's whole present centre unchanged medieval fabric. Say it **conserva** its medieval character, walls and historic features.
- The **ola almenada**, talking harbour and moving map are app fiction, labelled **NUESTRA HISTORIA**.
- CMV themes and hands-on/free-route concept are stable enough for story content. Opening days, exact turns, prices, capacity and exhibit availability are volatile.
- As checked on 1 October 2026, CMV's official winter-hours page lists two Tuesday–Friday public turns, `14:30–16:15` and `16:45–18:30`, and strongly recommends online booking. The family should verify the live booking calendar before choosing `verona-rain` for Friday 9 October.
- CMV pages differ slightly in wording: the FAQ says booking is not compulsory but strongly advised, while the rules describe visits as booked online or at the desk subject to capacity. Parent copy should say **“Aforo por turnos: comprueba disponibilidad y reserva antes de ir”**, not promise walk-in entry.
- The illustrated museum path is not a simulation of a named physical exhibit. Its water–mechanics–light chain is **NUESTRA HISTORIA**, built from verified theme areas.
- None of the three scored paths may depend on current weather, GPS, venue entry, a ticket, an operating exhibit or a real-world object being visible.

## Variant 1 — `borghetto-water-mill`

### Display title

**El hilo amarillo del molino**

### Opening narration

> Junto al Mincio, una rueda de molino ha quedado inmóvil. Entre sus palas, Bussola descubre un hilo de seda amarilla y dos recuerdos mezclados: uno pertenece a la historia de Borghetto; el otro, a una leyenda de Silvia y Malco. Para liberar la hebra tendremos que mover el agua, despertar el molino y colocar cada recuerdo bajo su nombre verdadero.

Primary CTA: **Despertar el molino**

Visual grammar: a blue paper river crosses the lower stage; a large wheel is visible at centre; a yellow thread links it to three sealed archive drawers: `HECHO`, `LEYENDA`, `NUESTRA HISTORIA`. The categories always use words, icons and different border patterns, never colour alone.

### Complete playable sequence

#### Scene B1 — Three drops

Three large drops hover above the stopped wheel. Each tap sends one drop into the paper river. Bussola counts, and the third turns the wheel.

**Prompt:** “Devuelve tres gotas al Mincio.”

No failure state. This is the four-year-old entry action and can be completed anywhere.

#### Scene B2 — How a mill remembers

Three illustrated cards appear: `AGUA`, `RUEDA`, `GRANO`. Their destination silhouettes and arrows are visible; the child places or taps them in order.

| Order | Card | Spoken resolve |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Water / `AGUA` | “El agua alcanza el molino.” |
| 2 | Wheel / `RUEDA` | “La rueda empieza a girar.” |
| 3 | Grain / `GRANO` | “Los antiguos molinos molían trigo y cereales.” |

Retry before water: “La rueda espera la corriente. Empieza con el agua.”

Retry before wheel: “El agua ya llegó. Ahora busca lo que gira.”

Retry before grain: “La rueda está despierta. ¿Qué molían estos molinos?”

After two retries the next silhouette pulses and Bussola demonstrates it. There is no score loss.

#### Scene B3 — The three drawers

Bussola reads each card before testing it:

1. **Molinos, casas de piedra y Ponte Visconteo** — documented place history.
2. **Silvia y Malco dejaron un paño amarillo anudado** — the Lover's Knot legend.
3. **Bussola encontró el hilo dentro de este mapa** — our app story.

**Prompt:** “Guarda cada recuerdo en su cajón.”

| Card ID | Correct drawer | Correct response |
|---|---|---|
| `mills-stone-bridge` | `HECHO` | “Quedan huellas físicas y fuentes que las describen.” |
| `silvia-malco-cloth` | `LEYENDA` | “Es el relato local del Nodo de Amor.” |
| `bussola-thread` | `NUESTRA HISTORIA` | “Eso ocurre en nuestra aventura.” |

Retry feedback names the distinction rather than saying only “incorrecto.” All three category titles stay visible throughout.

#### Scene B4 — The yellow-knot question

**Prompt:** “¿Qué tarjeta pertenece a la leyenda?”

| Choice ID | Label | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `legend-yellow-cloth` | **Silvia y Malco dejan una tela amarilla anudada** | correct |
| `fact-water-mills` | **Los molinos servían para moler trigo y cereales** | retry |
| `our-story-bussola` | **Bussola guarda el hilo dentro del Atlas** | retry |

Correct response: “Leyenda encontrada. El hilo amarillo ya puede salir de la rueda.”

Retry for the mill: “Eso pertenece a la historia documentada del lugar.”

Retry for Bussola: “Eso lo hemos inventado para esta aventura: es NUESTRA HISTORIA.”

#### Scene B5 — Optional Borghetto echo

Offer equal unscored buttons:

- **Abrir una ventana a Borghetto**
- **Seguir en el teatro ilustrado**

Only after the first is selected, show once:

> La pista se mira desde el paseo; no os acerquéis al borde del Mincio.

Then ask: **“¿Qué huella aparece desde donde ya estáis?”**

- **Una rueda** — “El molino conserva su silueta.”
- **Piedra o un arco** — “El puente y las casas hablan con piedra.”
- **Agua** — “El Mincio atraviesa la página real.”
- **Hoy no se distingue** — “El teatro ilustrado ya guarda todas las huellas.”

Every answer continues. No one confirms another child's observation and the app never asks the family to move.

#### Scene B6 — Release the thread

The child traces one short, wide path along the yellow thread. Reduced-motion and tap-only modes replace tracing with three large knot points. The thread leaves the wheel, loops once into the shape of a tortellino and becomes the passport seal.

### Completion and reward

> La rueda gira. Los tres cajones se cierran y el hilo amarillo cruza el Atlas sin confundir lo que ocurrió, lo que cuenta la leyenda y lo que inventamos nosotros. Bussola anuda la hebra alrededor del sello: **“Una buena historia no es menos mágica por llevar la etiqueta correcta.”**

**Success line:** “Molino, leyenda y aventura: cada recuerdo encontró su lugar.”

**Discovery fact:** “Los antiguos molinos de Borghetto molían trigo y cereales. El relato de Silvia, Malco y la seda amarilla es la leyenda del Nodo de Amor.”

## Variant 2 — `lazise-bonus-spotter`

### Display title

**La ciudad que tenía tres lados**

### Opening narration

> En el mapa del Garda, Lazise aparece como una ciudad de piedra a medio dibujar. Bussola levanta una muralla al norte, otra al este y otra al sur. En el oeste queda un vacío azul. **“No falta una pared”**, dice al ver una ola escondida. Para completar la ciudad tendremos que descubrir qué protegía su cuarto lado y devolverle la salida hacia el puerto.

Primary CTA: **Completar Lazise**

Visual grammar: top-down paper town, three textured wall edges and a blank west edge. A compass rose labels `OESTE` with both word and sunset icon. Gate counts never appear.

### Complete playable sequence

#### Scene L1 — Raise three walls

Three crenellated wall pieces appear one at a time. Tapping each raises it around the town and prints one, two, then three stone marks.

**Prompt:** “Levanta los tres lados de piedra.”

No failure state. The third tap reveals the open blue west side.

#### Scene L2 — The missing fourth edge

**Narration:**

> Las murallas scaligeras rodean Lazise por tres lados. Hacia el oeste, la ciudad se encuentra con el lago de Garda.

**Prompt:** “¿Qué completa el lado oeste?”

| Choice ID | Label / icon | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `lake-west` | **El lago** / wave and sunset | correct |
| `fourth-wall` | **Otra muralla igual** / stones | retry |
| `mountain-west` | **Una montaña** / peak | retry |

Correct response: “El lago completa el mapa sin convertirse en muralla.”

Retry: “Mira el vacío azul y la rosa de los vientos: el oeste toca el Garda.”

#### Scene L3 — Open the harbour vignette

The lake card folds down and reveals three fixed silhouettes from the municipal description: old harbour, Venetian Customs building and San Nicolò church. The child taps them in the app's illuminated order; no historical identification is required.

1. **Puerto antiguo** — “Aquí llegaba la ciudad al agua.”
2. **Dogana Veneta** — “Este edificio recuerda el comercio por el lago.”
3. **San Nicolò** — “La pequeña iglesia permanece junto al puerto.”

These are discovery captions, not a memory quiz. `Dogana Veneta` and `San Nicolò` can remain optional written labels for older children.

#### Scene L4 — Build the fixed emblem

Bussola presents two pieces already chosen by the app:

- one stone battlement from the wall;
- one blue wave from the lakefront.

**Prompt:** “Une la almena y la ola.”

The pieces interlock as an **ola almenada**. This replaces the current instruction to invent a team symbol.

Resolve: “Piedra y lago en un solo emblema: Lazise ha recuperado su cuarto lado.”

#### Scene L5 — Optional Lazise echo

Offer equal unscored buttons:

- **Abrir una ventana a Lazise**
- **Seguir en el teatro ilustrado**

Only after the first is selected, show once:

> En el puerto, observad desde el paseo.

Then ask: **“¿Qué parte del emblema aparece desde donde ya estáis?”**

- **Muralla o almena** — “La mitad de piedra está despierta.”
- **Ola o lago** — “La mitad azul toca la ciudad.”
- **Las dos** — “El emblema completo salió del Atlas.”
- **Hoy ninguna** — “El mapa ilustrado conserva las dos.”

All answers continue; there is no location or observation validation.

### Completion and reward

> La ola encaja en el borde occidental. El puerto se ilumina y la muralla deja de parecer incompleta. Bussola estampa el nuevo emblema sobre el mapa: **“Una ciudad no siempre termina donde acaba la piedra. A veces su último lado se mueve, brilla y lleva barcos.”** La ola almenada queda guardada en el pasaporte.

**Success line:** “Tres lados de piedra y un lado de agua completaron Lazise.”

**Discovery fact:** “Las murallas rodean tres lados de Lazise; al oeste, la ciudad histórica se abre al lago y a su antiguo puerto.”
